# Code of Conduct

This project is a place to learn. Many people here are new to Laravel or to programming,
so we want everyone to feel welcome and safe asking questions.

## Our standards

**Do**
- Be kind, patient and respectful
- Assume good intentions and explain things clearly
- Give constructive feedback on code, not on people
- Welcome beginners and "simple" questions

**Don't**
- Harass, insult or demean anyone
- Use sexual language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances
- Make personal or political attacks
- Share other people's private information without permission

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, please open a private report through GitHub
(**Report content** on the comment or issue) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
Reports will be handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
